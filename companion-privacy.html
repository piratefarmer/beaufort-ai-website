Companion Messaging Service — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy describes how the Companion Messaging Service ("the Service") handles information for participants. The Service is a wellness text-message companion program operated by Beaufort AI LLC ("Beaufort AI," "we," "us") for individuals who benefit from regular, low-effort conversational contact — for example, older adults or individuals in a care setting. Enrollment is arranged by a family member, legal guardian, or authorized caregiver on the participant's behalf.

Who participates

Participation is by direct invitation only. A family member or authorized caregiver sets up the Service on behalf of a participant, using the participant's own existing phone number, with the participant's knowledge and agreement. The Service does not accept public sign-ups and does not message any number that has not been set up this way.

What information is collected

  • Text messages sent and received between the participant and the Service.
  • Basic engagement information — for example, whether a scheduled check-in was answered.

The Service does not collect payment information, financial details, government ID numbers, or passwords, and participants are never asked to provide any of these.

Who can see this information

  • Family members / authorized caregivers who set up the Service receive summary-level information only — such as general engagement and wellbeing patterns and time-sensitive alerts — not full message transcripts, as a normal part of using the Service.
  • A treating physician or care provider, if the participant's family has arranged for one, may review full conversation transcripts on request, for care purposes.
  • The Service does not sell, rent, or share participant information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

What the Service does not do

  • It does not send marketing, promotional, or commercial messages of any kind.
  • It does not provide medical advice or make medication-related recommendations.
  • It does not request or process payment, banking, or financial information.
  • It is not a substitute for emergency services; participants are directed to contact on-site staff or emergency services directly for anything urgent.

Opting out

A participant or their family may stop using the Service at any time by contacting the family member who set it up, who will deactivate the number. Standard "STOP" text-message opt-out handling is also honored.

Data retention

Message data is retained only as long as needed to support the participant's ongoing use of the Service and, where applicable, care-related review by an authorized physician or caregiver. It is not retained for any other purpose.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Beaufort AI LLC at submissions@beaufortai.ai, or to the family member or caregiver who administers the Service for a specific participant.
